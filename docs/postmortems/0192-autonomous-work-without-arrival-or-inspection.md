Artifact-Version: 1

## Executive summary

Actual long-rest play exposed a local-work chain that talked about fetching records and counting stock without reaching the appropriate room. The canonical movements repeatedly remained in the hall, and later expressive manner was published as physical handling of an unestablished ledger. A second scene treated a route reference as an observed visitor. The fixes separate required execution sites, admitted physical expression, finite activity scope and reference-only knowledge.

## Root cause

The autonomous reading lacked the player's required-site check. The world outcome rule judged the selected kind and parameters independently of the concrete immediate attempt, so a valid movement to the current hall was accepted as the mechanism for a different named destination. Both the action and its expressive prose were then available as apparently successful receipts.

Perception copied raw manner whenever an action succeeded. The expression could therefore assert a fetched ledger or handover without a matching item, ownership or transfer effect. Speech may be mistaken; an engine-generated observation cannot treat a speaker's accompanying invented material act as independently verified.

The ongoing flag followed the proposed profile rather than the finite scope of the work. Furthermore, ongoing actions had a one-second initial admission and skipped total-duration adjudication, so merely clearing their flag would have made counting unrealistically instantaneous. The same batch determines scope and the relevant duration.

Authoring omitted ordinary professional knowledge of the premises, and the existing route-reference introduction described indirect knowledge as observed presence. Both defects made subsequent choices less coherent. Initial familiarity now has actual local bindings and experience evidence, while current stock still requires inspection. Relation-only references remain reported until actual visibility establishes a present object.
