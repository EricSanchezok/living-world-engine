# Scene Assumptions and Rejected Capability Annotations

Artifact-Version: 1

## Executive summary

Frozen narrative review found unsupported background people, possessions, player actions and speaker attribution in otherwise plausible paragraphs. A separate capability probe failed because a placement effect carried an extra descriptive field. Publication separates factual concerns inside one request; capability parsing ignores harmless annotations while validating executable fields.

## Summary

The retained `narrative-confirmation-1791929026076` cohort qualified 28 of 37 turns. Waiting invented workers, observation became pickup, a resource check invented a wallet, and a player's question became another actor's words. Continuous play also selected an older speaker after a shop search failed. In `narrative-regression-1791930115726`, folding an existing paper target failed twice because a placement effect included `description`; invocation `288eed66-10f5-4998-b49a-f684cae47067::rt:model-audit:a4f3e080b1cb0eabeca755a68adbd6dd1ec0e1db75b230c9aec15c1fcedf51cf` retains the rejected declaration.

## Timeline

The eighth confirmation cohort and sixth continuous session exposed material additions and confused recipients. The fourth witnessed-capability probe then retained two rejected annotated placement declarations. A same-scene publication probe reproduced missed additions and rejection of accurately attributed lies. The final correction separates the publication instruction profile, preserves typed capability effects, and freezes a fresh cohort.

## Root cause

A holistic prose judgment sharing the world adjudicator's common-sense policy accepted familiar story details without independently matching material objects, actors, event results and speech. Prior unsupported prose then became tempting continuity material. Current recipient questions buried the recent unsuccessful request. Strict capability object parsing rejected extra explanation that had no execution meaning. Repeating that explanation consumed the single repair without improving the action.

## Guardrails

[Publication regressions](../../src/server/__tests__/direct-world-host.test.ts) preserve an accepted prefix when one focused judgment rejects a later paragraph. [Narrative helpers](../../src/server/__tests__/world-narrative.test.ts) require all four factual foci; they remain one request per available paragraph batch. The narrator uses executed scene evidence and treats prior prose only as a style reference. Recipient selection includes recent requested referents even when the encounter failed. [Activity tests](../../src/engine/direct/__tests__/activities.test.ts) accept annotated placement effects without generating again. Extra fields cannot become state operations; required types, references and mechanical checks remain in force. [The narrative reference](../game-design/narrative.md) owns these boundaries.

The publication profile uses a closed evidence-comparison system instruction, including an explicit distinction between truth and accurately attributed delivered lies. World adjudication retains its common-sense authority. The [policy regression](../../src/server/__tests__/world-narrative.test.ts) prevents these policies from sharing a prompt version.
