# Direct Action Projections Lost Conversation Evidence

Artifact-Version: 1

## Executive summary

The first live direct-runtime canaries reduced model requests but failed to sustain conversation. Action construction allowed speaking without a known recipient, compressed unrelated questions into a goods-request template, and mixed immediate speech with fulfillment of the larger goal. Private context also omitted older unlinked evidence after advancing the observation cursor.

## Summary

The frozen `8f4aba67` producer completed its host runs and replayed its states, but did not establish the requested accommodation conversation or station repair. Both Jev and DeepSeek decision arms exhibited missing recipients. These terminal runs are incomplete tasks. The [direct-runtime research record](../research/2026-10-13-jev-direct-world-runtime.md) owns the measurements and budget.

## Root cause

The mocks supplied mutually consistent parameter choices, hiding the fact that independently answered questions lacked branch-specific meanings. The world request preserved the whole user intent without clearly separating the current action. Context filtering treated an observation cursor as an expiration time for knowledge. A successful host run and a correct replay could therefore coexist with unusable feedback.

## Guardrails

[Action regressions](../../src/engine/direct/__tests__/runtime.test.ts) cover recipient discovery, original-request continuation, exact speech spans and owned inventory. [Activity regressions](../../src/engine/direct/__tests__/activities.test.ts) preserve the inspector's private code after new observations and retain the engineer's isolation. [WorldHost regressions](../../src/server/__tests__/direct-world-host.test.ts) verify actual delivery, refusals and retryable local player failures without unrelated NPC work. [The frozen qualification protocol](../../scripts/experiments/jev-qualification-protocol.ts) requires reviewing delivered evidence and persistent state; a completed run, low request count or identical replay cannot establish task success.

The activity regressions also require present-time NPC waits to preserve future timers, current fact descriptions after deterministic and random changes, a separate mechanical random commitment before its draw, and immutable shared provenance with multiple bindings. These mechanics do not claim that a real model will always choose the appropriate branch; live semantic review remains required.

Subsequent live canaries exposed missing inventory scope and private reflection operations. Their regressions distinguish inventory from scene discovery, record synthesis as private inference without world adjudication, and project recognizable public names without granting access to restricted facts. The model's perceptibility answer cannot bypass canonical fact access.

A later real probe selected reflection correctly but answered the speech question with no words, so no requested synthesis was produced. A plausible new marker action also had no corresponding state property. Private synthesis therefore has its own conditional question, and world adjudication can request a concise missing effect declaration after ordinary action selection. The extension regression checks its observed effect and another actor's reuse without another creation request.
