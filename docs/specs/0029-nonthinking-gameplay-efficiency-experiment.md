# Non-thinking Gameplay Efficiency Experiment

Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Improve credible continuous gameplay by removing measured avoidable model work, cache-prefix loss, recovery defects and semantic collapse. The user delegates experiment design and execution, authorizes a separate CNY 1000, requires thinking disabled, and replaces the fixed time window with outcome-based completion. The single research and execution record owns evolving measurements and frozen trial designs. Work remains on the current branch with local verified commits.

## Contract

STEP-E2 uses an independent evidence root and append-only budget. STEP-E1 charges, unknown reservations, stopped trials and source data remain intact and cannot be transferred or refunded through the new authorization. Every paid request binds its actual model, account, price snapshot, request/response hashes, usage and disposition. Missing usage retains its full reservation. The shared budget implementation remains authoritative.

All E2 model requests use the configured DeepSeek Flash model with thinking explicitly disabled at the actual HTTP boundary. Low effort is not disabled thinking. Missing or contradictory controls fail before reservation and network access. A response reporting reasoning work stops further sends after any reliable usage is settled; it does not erase incurred cost or fabricate unknown billing. There is no automatic model substitution, stronger-model escalation or thinking fallback.

Experiments preserve natural-language actions, complete world and logical batch cardinality, canonical/private-state separation, deterministic rules, reference permissions, atomic commits and bounded recovery. Model-facing semantic clarification, reversible context layout, compact typed representations and exact-result reuse require separately frozen paired trials. No extra online planner, critic or voting calls are introduced. Reuse requires a proven binding to the same authorized inputs and rules; similar text or an unchanged actor ID is insufficient. Component observations and merged-world observations are separate responsibilities until their equivalence is established.

Formal validity, reference validity, original-action semantics, state effects and efficiency are separate outcomes. A generated plan cannot serve as its own independent semantic oracle. Known-effect scenarios bind preconditions, expected changes or continuing/waiting/blocked behavior to authored rules and source actions. All-brief and all-no-effect mutants must fail cases requiring duration or persistent effects, while legitimate observation, waiting, already-satisfied goals and blocked actions may have no physical delta. A short world step does not imply that every activity completes at that boundary.

Each paid comparison freezes inputs, prompt/code hashes, model settings, batch size, repetition count, ordering, prices, stop conditions and its admission rule before its first send. A failed stage stops that trial and produces partial evidence. Subsequent optimization uses a new trial identity. Per-request and per-step watchdogs bound an individual experiment without creating an overall goal deadline. No historical automation resumes automatically.

Full-world acceptance requires at least three successive real WorldHost commits and a fresh independently initialized confirmation trajectory, increasing step/revision/time, canonical replay equality, and source-bound checks of meaningful effects, persistence and later events. An observed false completion or attribution failure vetoes gameplay acceptance even after a commit. Report initial and recovery calls, physical HTTP concurrency, cache hit/miss, reasoning tokens, latency and total charges including failed trials. Overlapping cost labels cannot be added as disjoint savings. Local interface testing must also verify the usable workbench and actual instance/profile bindings.

## Plan

Continue diagnosis and optimization while the goal remains unmet and the authorization permits useful work.
