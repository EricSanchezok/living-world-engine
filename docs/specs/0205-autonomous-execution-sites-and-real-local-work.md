# Autonomous execution sites and real local work

Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Repair a live local-work chain that repeatedly claimed travel to a storeroom while its canonical placement remained in the hall. The user's 2026-10-17 instruction to finish unfinished work and repair actual play, followed by the integration review's explicit request to fix the evidenced cognition and move/observe continuation defects, authorizes this generic correction. Speech may remain mistaken or deceptive; engine receipts must describe the actual committed step.

## Contract

- Apply the current-site boundary and required-site reading from [Spec 0202](0202-action-execution-site-binding.md) to autonomous actions as well as player actions. Local identity keys remain private to the acting subject. A different named room is not silently identified with the current place.
- A move's bound destination must agree with its required execution site. A known different required site cannot execute a nonmovement method before arrival. A genuinely unknown site does not authorize a move to the current place labeled as arrival elsewhere; an autonomous character may seek directions, investigate at their actual location or wait for information using ordinary actions.
- Reuse the existing bounded consistency repair and settlement path. The shared reading introduces no mandatory model call, no special-world branch, no migration and no automatic travel based on a target label. Autonomous intent and player authorization remain distinct.
- Scripted professional or household familiarity is represented with actual local identities, bindings and evidence. Knowing the shop or storeroom does not disclose its present inventory remotely. Inspection of accessible stock must measure its existing quantity; it neither creates stock nor performs a narrated handover.
- The existing world batch determines whether a proposed ongoing activity has a finite scope or is deliberately sustained. Finite inspections receive a realistic total duration; sustained work receives its initial setup time and retains the existing interruption path. A careful or repetitive finite count does not remain active indefinitely. Observation may be sustained when monitoring is genuinely chosen.
- The world outcome checks whether bound parameters implement the concrete immediate attempt. It does not require completion of later compound work, and does not award partial success for substituting a different activity. Nonempty manner receives a conditional admission in that same batch. Prepared actions retain required `visibleManner` separately from the original attempted expression; only supported manner of an actually successful execution enters perception. Speech may remain mistaken or deceptive. [Decision 0263](../decisions/0263-admit-physical-expression-with-world-semantics.md) records the alternatives.
- A fact pointing to another entity introduces only a reported reference unless actual perception separately admits that entity. The existing physical visibility and name-recognition decisions may reveal a visible type or recognizable name, without exposing hidden canonical identity. Seeing no arrival is not evidence of absence.
- Publish this behavior as `direct-world@14` under execution contract v15 with direct control v6. Required preparation fields reject stale state directly; outer script, simulation and instance formats are v19, v23 and v36.
