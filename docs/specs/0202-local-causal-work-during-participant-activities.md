# Local causal work during participant activities

Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Keep relevant local affairs evolving while a participant performs a long activity. The user's 2026-10-17 instruction to finish all work and repair actual play authorizes this generic scheduling correction. Real overnight rest reached the correct dawn while nearby characters remained ready to answer a sixty-second offer. Faithful elapsed time alone does not establish a living world.

## Contract

- An unfinished participant activity is a concurrent activity, not exclusive ownership of world time. Ready local work and its active causal consequences must receive interpretation before the clock skips beyond their opportunity to act. A nearby person's response to another nearby person is not excluded merely because its immediate cause was not the participant's submission.
- Eligibility comes from actual locality, established ongoing work and causal links. Unrelated globally ready characters are not all activated for each foreground boundary. Existing private perspectives and canonical authority remain separate; eligibility does not grant another character's knowledge.
- A local activity window is anchored to the unfinished participant activity or submission. It includes nearby actors with genuine opportunities and preserves follow-through for admitted local work whose next step temporarily leaves the immediate place. A mere historic encounter does not permanently attach every future action of that actor to the window. Window membership ends with the root work and does not manufacture a new goal or obligation.
- Existing direct participant reactions retain their causal identity and urgency, but cannot indefinitely starve other already eligible local work. Independent eligible interpretations share the existing batch opportunity. The scheduler does not lower batch cardinality or add a separate semantic scheduling invocation.
- Every admitted action still requires ordinary interpretation, preparation, duration, settlement and perceptual delivery. Elapsed rest cannot silently execute guessed negotiations, purchases, travel, cultivation or task completion. Character refusal, silence and motivated waiting remain valid choices.
- A character that explicitly waits for evidence or a deadline can become quiescent. Existing repeated-no-progress safeguards remain applicable. Readiness must not be discarded merely to make a requested time jump cheap, nor repeatedly awakened without a changed cause or a real due condition.
- The root deadline and stopping condition remain fixed while local actions evolve. A delivered qualifying interruption cancels only the unfinished tail. A publication for an earlier event retains its earlier state. Restart and recorded replay preserve the eligible local work and its consequences.
- Unsupported service or semantic failures remain visible failures, rather than successful time advancement past omitted work. No world-specific actor IDs, chapter events, fixed universal daily routines or unconditional globally active simulation are introduced.

## Plan

Trace the existing readiness, foreground reaction and locality paths at the failed boundary. Retain the priority and authority of explicit participant interpretation, then admit relevant ready local continuations alongside direct reactions while root work is active. Derive membership from existing stable causal records where possible; if the local window needs explicit persisted bookkeeping, keep it in the versioned direct runtime contract with exact validation and no compatibility path. Compare local continuation with transitive direct-reaction closure and unconditional global readiness before selecting the smallest mechanism that covers both the failure and temporary local follow-through.

## Verification

Reproduce through the real engine: a participant rests, a local buyer addresses a local seller, and the seller becomes ready before the rest deadline. The seller must get a decision and its committed response must occur before dawn; merely leaving a ready intent at the deadline is failure. Test simultaneous direct participant reactions without starving that exchange, a related short departure and return, an unrelated distant ready actor, a genuine wait for missing evidence, and repeated-no-progress termination. Verify no model knowledge leakage, no fabricated transaction, exact root time, a delivered interruption, restart and replay.

Run the original human overnight scenario and the spirit-room scenario with actual models. Read their full action and narrative trajectories, not just final clocks. Record actual participating actors, invocation and token totals, queue and transport time, first rejection, repair and terminal failure separately. Compare costs per resulting local action and end-to-end latency with the preserved failed run; additional causally necessary actions are not equivalent to redundant scheduling calls. The world-scale acceptance remains separate from these local regressions.
