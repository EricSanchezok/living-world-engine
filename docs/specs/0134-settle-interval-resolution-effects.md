# Settle interval resolution effects

Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Preserve adjudicated consequences during continuing Activities. The continuing player-efficiency authorization covers this execution-contract correction; model latency and complete-player qualification remain separate obligations.

## Contract

Every ResolutionPlan adjudicates the supplied current temporal interval. Its receipt applies the engine-derived effects exactly once in the same atomic step, including when the complete source action remains continuing. A receipt grade describes that interval's resolution and does not prove that the full task has completed. The transition must retain one outcome per assigned action. A continuing outcome requires the actual Activity context; terminal outcomes retain the receipt-grade constraint and the existing fixed, rate, staged, goal and conditional completion rules.

The kernel rejects an unconsumed interval receipt, altered check or effect transcript, duplicate settlement, and unsupported temporal outcome. Supported interruption can terminate an Activity after a continuing interval outcome without discarding that interval's effects. Resource-deferred actions remain excluded from adjudication. No plan or roll is carried to another boundary, reinterpreted, or silently rerolled. Independent interval uncertainties may still require independent checks; this correction does not remove those calls.

Every plan-review context includes the exact temporal boundary, independently of optional detailed Activity evidence. Plan review must reject effects that require a later task completion or refer to another interval. Final causal review still evaluates the actual effects against the full source, world laws and boundary. A future completion effect is represented when its prerequisites actually hold, not applied early or automatically classified from prose by the kernel. The change does not claim that model semantic review is infallible. Default runtime compatibility advances, obsolete saves are not migrated, and historical recorded experiments remain immutable.

## Plan

Separate interval receipt consumption from Activity lifecycle in TruthEngine and transaction verification, align model instructions and the owning temporal documentation, and advance producer contracts. Preserve exact check derivation and trusted mechanics. Record the reproduced lost-effects failure and its permanent real-entry guard.

## Verification

Use the real world loader, external player entry, SimulationEngine, CanonicalCommitter and replay with only model boundaries replaced. Exercise a three-second Activity with a required effect each second: every committed check must consume its own receipt, health must change at each boundary, the Activity must remain active before its third second, and all three steps must replay. Include failed-check consequences, early completion and future-effect vetoes, tampered receipt/check transcripts and unchanged state on rejection. Retain existing partial placement, goal, conditional, resource, timing and resolution suites. Run focused tests and check:fast before a local commit. A controlled fixture is neither a live semantic accuracy score nor evidence for the sixty-second target.
