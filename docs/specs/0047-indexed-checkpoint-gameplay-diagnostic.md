## Intent

## Contract

The explicit candidate Composition combines the source-indexed planning wire from [0044](0044-source-indexed-planning-records.md), source-intent review from [0046](0046-plan-review-source-intent-diagnostic.md), and complete temporal evidence. It retains the dependent codec, original source inventory, fixed repair limits, complete physical batches and shared-state-first transport. The review instruction runs at the logical verifier boundary before batching; indexing runs at the physical planning boundary after the worklist projection. Prompt and codec identities are pinned and unknown combinations reject. Default Compositions remain unchanged.

The launcher selects the immutable full checkpoint world from [0045](0045-short-action-checkpoint-world.md). It requires the frozen 45/3 mechanical qualification and the first-response twelve-case source-intent diagnostic. It records the original 045 semantic rejection explicitly; that candidate remains rejected and its plans are not re-reviewed. This new treatment changes the reviewer and world contract. Its fresh plans must pass the actual runtime's mandatory formal and semantic gates before commitment. Historical acceptance does not substitute for those gates, and development controls do not certify general semantics.

This is an integrated diagnostic in a new isolated save, not default rollout or retrospective admission of a failed trial. Keep all 48 Agents and 232 entities, action text, source meaning, models with thinking disabled, and full context. No extra online critic, stronger model or transport resampling is added. Existing watchdogs, append-only E2 accounting, unknown-billing reservations and partial reports remain authoritative. All actual plan/review/repair calls belong to runtime costs; historical development costs remain separate.

## Plan

Register the fully pinned candidate and verify its actual SimulationEngine entry path. Prepare a fresh trajectory using the normal loader, catalog import, WorldHost, Ledger and replay. Freeze code, Composition, source-world hashes, models, budgets and stop criteria before the first paid call. After every actual commit, require the existing source-bound assistant review before the next step. Failed or unknown source behavior stops the trial and preserves its evidence. Three credible successive steps and a fresh confirmation remain necessary for gameplay acceptance.

## Verification

Exercise registration drift rejection, missing foundations, canonical decoding, unchanged original goals, logical source-intent review placement, physical indexed output, complete source/temporal evidence and canonical replay. Check that source or diagnostic-report tampering cannot open the launcher. Inspect every committed source action for premature success, misattribution, lost prerequisites, unsupported effects and empty-effect non-progress. Measure useful time/progress per step and all HTTP, tokens, cache, latency and cost; goal persistence alone is not success. Run relevant tests and check:fast before commit. No comparative improvement claim follows from historical failed worlds as an unpaired baseline.
