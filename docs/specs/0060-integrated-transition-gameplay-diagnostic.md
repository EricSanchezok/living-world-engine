Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Exercise the complete existing engine after resolving measured structural transition failures, within the delegated [gameplay experiment](0029-nonthinking-gameplay-efficiency-experiment.md). The candidate combines indexed planning and source-intent review with indexed transition outcomes, nonempty assertion encoding and lossless candidate layouts. [Decision 0139](../decisions/0139-test-mechanical-transition-integration.md) defines the selection boundary.

## Contract

The registered indexed-reviewed truth-resolution candidate advances to version 2 with a new pinned pipeline identity and prompt fingerprint. Its transition path uses the canonical summary and effect contract through the indexed, evidence-worklist, catalog-prefix and catalog-record adapters. Singleton transitions retain the canonical single-request path. Algorithm, batching, model and prompt identities are recorded in each new instance; no old instance is migrated or silently reassigned.

The experimental intervalAssessment interface is explicitly selected only by the independent research probe. Its gate, currentWork and pendingSourceRanges generation has not qualified for runtime integration. Keep its implementation and failed evidence as an isolated research interface, while the gameplay provider has one selected mechanical transition path. Do not reinterpret historical semantic failures as passes or feed their outputs into a new world.

The candidate retains the engine's existing causal verifier, observation validation and bounded repair. No new online critic, planner, voting call or stronger inference is added. Formal success of a raw transition is only an intermediate result. A fresh integrated diagnostic can test recovery of semantic errors; accepted gameplay requires the complete existing pipeline and independent source-bound inspection of committed state and behavior. A definite error or unknown result at that final boundary vetoes acceptance and stops further steps. No raw intermediate semantic result qualifies the candidate for default interactive use.

Every actual non-thinking experiment request checks the complete HTTP body with the pinned tokenizer before entering the existing reservation/dispatch boundary. A context failure stops new requests, records its evidence and incurs no new HTTP charge. Preserve the original model and output settings, full world and action scope, all existing costs and unknown reservations.
