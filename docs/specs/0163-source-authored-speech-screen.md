# Source-Authored Speech Screen

Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Test whether a decision-time author can choose exact current speech without reconstructing the speaker, utterance or temporal scope from a later paraphrase. This prerequisite screen serves the [complete-player objective](0122-player-action-efficiency.md); it is not a promoted communication transport or a promise of deterministic perception.

## Contract

Each experimental nextActionIntent contains kind, text and the existing ordered targetHandles. Open retains the complete unrestricted intended action, including compound, conditional, parallel and ongoing work. Speak denotes exactly the words the source character intends to attempt aloud at its current place now. Orders, falsehoods and conditional quotations remain speech contents, not executed effects. If the chosen action includes travel, waiting, delivery or concurrent physical work, the open path remains available without a reduced vocabulary. No subject is required to choose speech.

Open decodes to rawText and goal equal to text, with means null. Speak uses a versioned JSON embedding preserving every utterance character, with the same goal and means convention. Existing AgentMind validates original local targets and private cognition. A diagnostic inspector consumes both the declared source choice and the materialized action, binds speaker identity from actorId, and exposes intended local addressees. It never infers a speech kind merely from arbitrary raw text. The resulting descriptor is explicitly unadjudicated; it cannot authorize reception, update beliefs, execute quoted instructions, settle a check or modify the world. No speech receipt, renderer, default runtime or external-player contract adopts this representation in the screen.

Ordinary speech needs an evidenced channel, and same placement or a selected target alone does not supply it. Magic, concealment, absent participants and contested audibility retain world adjudication. This screen does not add an all-hearing location rule or reinterpret prose world laws as executable preconditions.

## Plan

Freeze the complete six original bootstrap batches of eight autonomous subjects, twelve alternating B/C first responses, official deepseek-flash with thinking disabled, and no model repairs or transport retries. B is the existing single-text producer with exact prior physical requests. C is the source-authored speech producer. Keep all complete private contexts, non-action fields and canonical validators. Different autonomous choices are allowed; historical text is not a gold action. A missing billable audit stops subsequent dispatch.

Preflight with complete historical-text fixtures under open and verify unchanged private patches, targets and materialized actions. Separately exercise speak and malformed outputs through the actual gateway and AgentMind. Review every generated action for source-supported identity, situation, audience, temporal interpretation and chosen content before any downstream qualification. Report open/speak coverage, rejected batches, token/cache/call costs and unchanged world state. These forty-eight-subject decisions do not constitute a forty-nine-subject player action.

## Verification

Test exact text preservation, code-bound speaker identity, explicit unadjudicated delivery, conditional utterances, unrestricted compound open actions and raw-text marker collisions. Verify original slot ownership, foreign local-target rejection, unknown fields, malformed text and complete rejected-output audits at the real gateway boundary. Validate frozen actual HTTP requests and run relevant checks plus check:fast before a local commit and paid freeze.
