# Canonical world authoring and participation seeds

Artifact-Version: 1
Status: Approved

## Intent

Implement the user's explicitly approved 2026-10-17 Fanren plan: a traceable human-world and spirit-world setting at the opening night of chapter one, eight original Origins, the single existing Han Li as an optional identity, a runnable world, and documented content and gameplay qualification. The user's instruction to implement that plan is the approval for this contract. The immortal-world sequel and predetermined future plot are outside scope.

## Contract

- Preserve the entire main novel's chapter inventory, source fingerprints, acquisition and review states, and discrepancies. Raw copyrighted text stays in the ignored local source cache. A fetched chapter is not a read, extracted, or reviewed chapter. A preview is not a full chapter.
- Human and spirit worlds each retain explicit coverage for geography, rules and exceptions, society and resources, people and relationships, and history. Completion of one region cannot satisfy the whole-world acceptance condition.
- The shared initial instant is the opening night before Han Li's uncle proposes admission to his family. Later descriptions require temporal assessment before entering that snapshot. Historical, deceased, unborn and unlocated subjects remain in the authoring index without automatically becoming living actors.
- Every important authored fact has a stable identity, source locator and version, truth interval, disclosure time, classification, dependencies and review state. Canon, testimony, back-projection, original additions and numerical calibration remain distinguishable.
- Reading batches contain at most eight chapters and approximately 20,000 Chinese characters; long chapters use scene-sized parts. Each batch produces facts, identity and temporal evidence, unresolved questions, and a second source review. Resume from the durable ledgers.
- Original Origins initialize real assets, relations, obligations and individual cognition in one validated initialization. Existing NPCs' knowledge of the newcomer is explicitly authored. Private canonical bindings never enter ordinary player or Agent views.
- Existing-identity entry controls one authored Agent, without cloning it or granting future possessions. Player customization cannot alter canonical cultivation, property or history. Both entry modes are generic engine capabilities.
- The eight original identities are village herbal apprentice, wealthy merchant heir, mundane escort apprentice, minor cultivation-clan branch, foundation independent cultivator, core-formation clan elder, spirit-world transformation merchant and spirit-world void-refinement array practitioner. Realm, wealth, kinship and special opportunities must exist in state. Clan and household property do not become personal inventory merely through biography.
- Unknown spirit-root status conceals a pre-established fact. The rootless mundane Origin has no promised cultivation unlock. High-level Origins retain their abilities and resources.
- The deterministic build uses reviewed authoring records; it does not invent missing canon. Future events depend on conditions and actions. Timers can wake actors but cannot enforce novel outcomes.
- Fidelity qualification disables free scene expansion. Approximately 150 and 700 participants are stress tiers, not population caps; complete-world qualification remains separate.
- Complete-world execution preserves every finite property alternative when its population exceeds a provider's per-question choice limit. A lossless same-batch representation must retain exact value selection, the existing open-value permission boundary, and one settlement dispatch; neither population nor candidate truncation can qualify the full package.
- The complete package retains one YAML file per entity. Its 5,164 authored entities require 5,168 archive entries, above the earlier 5,000-entry import ceiling. The generic import ceiling is 10,000 entries, checked before extraction; the independent 50 MiB compressed and 100 MiB expanded limits and all path, link, duplicate-name and single-world checks remain unchanged. This bounded capacity change is required by the approved full-world import, rather than removing content or introducing a second file format.
