# Local playability acceptance before promotion

## Status
Accepted
Class: testing

## Context and Problem Statement

The action compiler has shared reference and fact-value fixes, registered R5 retrieval, and a reversible T conditional representation. Offline compiler acceptance does not establish that world execution reaches temporal resolution, cognition, observation and an atomic state commit. Calibrated open-language evaluation is also insufficient to certify semantic fidelity.

## Decision Drivers

- Establish usable continuous world execution within a bounded cost.
- Preserve the authored world, action freedom, batch sizes and strict commits.
- Keep a candidate's identity reproducible without prematurely changing every new instance.
- Separate smoke acceptance from comparative performance or semantic claims.

## Considered Options

- Continue offline optimization until a semantic evaluator passes calibration.
- Promote several experimental representations and transports together.
- Pin B1 repairs, R5 retrieval and T representation to one local acceptance instance.

## Decision Outcome

Use the registered T composition for a new persisted local acceptance instance through the real WorldHost entry path. The shared B1 fixes and R5 retrieval remain active. The global default remains unchanged until gameplay evidence supports promotion. Preserve the complete bundled world and profile settings. Record three consecutive state commits, positive engine-owned time advancement, actual requests, tokens, costs and elapsed time. The dedicated runner reserves at most CNY 50 from the user's remaining authorized budget, keeps a separate journal and raw HTTP evidence, and stops on uncertain billing. Its serialized transport means elapsed times are smoke measurements rather than production concurrency comparisons. AC-FP3 remains stopped under its own protocol.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Continue offline evaluation

- Good: develops stronger semantic evidence.
- Bad: leaves the user's immediate playability question unanswered.

### Promote multiple changes together

- Good: may combine several useful effects.
- Bad: complicates failure attribution and promotes unverified integration.

### Pin one acceptance instance (selected)

- Good: exercises the complete runtime with reversible local selection and durable evidence.
- Bad: one world and a short trajectory cannot establish general reliability; serial budget enforcement limits latency interpretation.
