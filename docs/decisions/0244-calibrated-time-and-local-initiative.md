# Calibrated time and local initiative

## Status
Accepted
Class: architecture

## Context and Problem Statement

Natural-language play needs plausible elapsed time, sufficient perceptible feedback and actors with their own purposes. Choosing the shortest authored duration for missing timing and prioritizing only external submissions suppress these qualities. Expanding narrator freedom alone cannot establish executable consequences.

## Decision Drivers

- Preserve player agency, private cognition, atomic commits and replay.
- Improve ordinary play across scripts without adding a scene-specific workflow.
- Let actual activity consume time while human reading and composition remain free.
- Keep extra inference proportional to relevant world work.

## Considered Options

- Tune narration alone while retaining minimum-duration and player-only scheduling.
- Add a separate director and continuous global background simulation.
- Calibrate time and perception in existing adjudication, with bounded local NPC admission alongside fresh player actions.

## Decision Outcome

Use existing adjudication for unresolved duration and perceptible context, and admit relevant ready NPCs at the start of a player request. Positive prompt criteria permit natural completion and motivated initiative without requiring drama on every turn. Explicit duration and authored procedures remain authoritative. Local creation, perceptual access and publication verification retain their existing owners.

The direct causal foreground still owns immediate replies and fulfillment. Other admitted activities persist on the shared timeline without indefinitely withholding the next player decision. No timer runs merely because the human is reading. Producer identity and a fresh data root isolate the behavior contract from archived saves.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

- Narration-only tuning is inexpensive but cannot repair time or give NPCs actual opportunities; richer prose could imply uncommitted events.
- A director can regulate dramatic pacing but adds another authority, more inference and new persistence concerns; it exceeds this experience-tuning scope.
- Existing-call calibration and bounded admission preserve ownership and allow quiet scenes. They still depend on model judgment and authoring quality, and relevant NPC decisions incur measurable inference cost.
