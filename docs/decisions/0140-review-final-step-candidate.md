# Review the Final Step Candidate

## Status

Accepted
Class: feature

## Context and Problem Statement

A component can pass causal review while a subsequent global observation render produces different statements. Component acceptance does not cover a later merged proposal or activity settlement. The nonthinking gameplay objective requires complete evidence and bounded repair without repeating preparation or buying reliability through additional critics.

## Decision Drivers

Exact final-content coverage, full action freedom, atomic rejection, stable random commitments, and lower duplicate observation and review work.

## Considered Options

- Keep component reviews and add a final critic.
- Review the complete final candidate in every component slot with explicit overlapping responsibilities.
- Replace component candidate reviews with one complete final-step review by the existing causal-verifier role.

## Decision Outcome

The existing causal-verifier role reviews the complete final step after observation and temporal settlement. Component preparation remains resumable for targeted repair. The canonical committer checks a content binding for the accepted candidate. [Specification 0075](../specs/0075-final-step-causal-review.md) owns the contract and verification.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

Adding a critic preserves redundant work and increases calls. Repeating the full candidate in component slots preserves local review granularity but duplicates evidence and requires a separate responsibility protocol. One final review eliminates overlapping candidate reviews and checks interactions explicitly, but enlarges the assigned action set and can change model reliability. Its paid performance remains an experimental question; exact content coverage is an engineering guarantee, not semantic proof.
