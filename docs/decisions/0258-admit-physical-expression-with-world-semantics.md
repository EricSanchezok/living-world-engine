# Admit physical expression with world semantics

## Status

Accepted
Class: bug-fix

## Context and Problem Statement

An action reader can correctly choose movement while writing a different destination, a completed inspection or a handover into its expression. Mechanical validation proves references exist and effects conserve resources; it does not prove those references implement the intended immediate method. Copying every expressive manner into perception also grants unexecuted material claims the authority of observation. A proposed ongoing temporal profile can similarly turn a finite inspection into indefinitely active work.

## Decision Drivers

- Keep character intentions distinct from world-admitted effects and perceived events.
- Preserve ordinary expressive gestures, motivated false speech and genuinely sustained activity.
- Resolve related semantics together without another mandatory model call or smaller batches.
- Retain the original attempted expression and decisions for durable replay and diagnosis.

## Considered Options

- Trust the action reader's binding and copy every manner into perception.
- Add a separate model validation call for each action or expressive clause.
- Add conditional criteria to the existing world batch and persist admitted visible manner.
- Delete manner and force every expressive gesture into a separate state-changing action.

## Decision Outcome

The existing world batch jointly checks whether bound parameters implement the immediate attempt, whether a nonempty manner is physically supported, and whether a proposed ongoing activity actually has a finite scope. The transaction kernel retains ownership of effects, references and arithmetic. The prepared action stores admitted `visibleManner` separately from the immutable attempted expression. Only a successful physical execution can publish it. A proposal that substitutes the current hall for an unknown account room admits no movement, even if an unrelated movement inside the hall would be easy.

The execution-site reading applies to player and autonomous actions. It detects contradictions before adjudication but does not replace the world check. A finite activity receives its own realistic duration; genuinely sustained work has an initial setup duration and remains active under existing interruption semantics. A relation reference alone introduces a reported object, without claiming its physical presence or private name. Actual entity visibility and name recognition remain independent admissions.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

Trusting the reader costs least but allows prose to bypass the strict effects path. Separate validation could isolate each clause but adds mandatory latency, duplicate context and more boundaries that can disagree. Conditional criteria reuse the complete local world and conflict component already being judged, at the cost of modest additional input and output within that call. They retain semantic uncertainty rather than replacing it with keyword rules.

Deleting manner would prevent this channel of unsupported claims, but would also remove harmless expressive delivery and turn natural gestures into needless turns. Keeping an admitted projection preserves useful expression while leaving the full rejected attempt in the audit trail.
