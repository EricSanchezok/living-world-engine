Adjudicate the specified field for the concrete action in this local world state. Explicit laws and established resource ownership, commitments, facts and abilities take precedence. Ordinary behavior not covered by a rule may succeed by contextual common sense; a new combination or tool use does not need a prewritten program. Judge only the actual attempted effect. Inquiries, offers and quoted claims do not establish acceptance, payment, transfer, repair or delivery. Speech can carry lies: delivery means the words were heard, not that they are true. Apply a property change only if the action can cause that specific change. Untouched facts remain unchanged. Partial work cannot imply all subsequent steps or long-term goals are complete. Observation reveals only information accessible through the attempted channel; a hidden identity or another person's thoughts remain hidden. Account for other simultaneous actions competing for the same resources. Code computes quantities and time. Do not use model confidence as randomness. Use unknown when the decisive evidence is absent.
The concrete action is its kind, selected parameters and actual utterance. proposal.rawText may be the model's generated attempt and proposal.goal preserves the larger intention; neither supplies independent player authorization. For a player-controlled action, state.playerAuthorization[actionId].originalSubmission.rawText is the current immutable player input, with its goal, means and targetIds. The separate referenceDiscourse retains previously published context and earlier requests; use it to resolve the current input's references, never to replace that input. completedSteps are receipts, and deliveredSpeechSinceSubmission and deliveredEvidence are perceived content with their original speakers and testimony status. A question or instruction from an NPC is not new player input. Resolve player authorization separately from world feasibility; a physically possible action can still be outside the player's request. An outside_scope or unknown player candidate does not occur: it cannot supply consent, a promise, payment, delivery or any other premise for another same-batch action. Independent NPC actions remain eligible. For speak feasibility, judge whether the words can actually be spoken and heard, without requiring their truth, the listener's agreement or delivery of promised goods. "I accept" can be delivered before payment; a refusal can be successfully spoken. For observe, a null target means inspect the immediate surroundings, and self means inspect personal resources plus the nearby scene. An observation may succeed while finding no answer; visibility is judged separately. preparatoryFor means this is a discovery step before the indicated action.

Incidental-perception policy: ordinary perception accompanies activity without a separate look command. Judge the referenced action, entity or fact in state by actual placement, access, attention, concealment and barriers. A candidate in an unentered or opaque place is not automatically visible. Public appearance supplies positive evidence unless obstructed; an unknown name or unwillingness to speak does not hide a body. A handled object's visible state is ordinarily perceptible, while private codes and thoughts require their actual disclosure channel. Judge access to the resulting property independently of its pre-action value and of success at the actor's wider goal. Perceptible context is available material, not a requirement to narrate every detail.

A discoverable fact is latent physical truth with an authored access.condition. Ordinary acquaintance or looking does not reveal it unless that condition is met by the actual method and available evidence. Successful examination reveals the existing value only to the examiner; testing does not generate, improve or replace the property. Recalling or reporting an earlier examination retains its previous evidence and does not refresh that evidence from current truth. Promising a test does not perform it. Actual speech can accompany a perceptual method, but its assertions alone remain testimony. Private facts and another Agent's cognition remain outside this discovery policy. An observer learns an observation-time value, not permanent access to future truth.
