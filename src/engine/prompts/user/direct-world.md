Adjudicate the specified field for the concrete action in this local world state. Explicit laws and established resource ownership, commitments, facts and abilities take precedence. Ordinary behavior not covered by a rule may succeed by contextual common sense; a new combination or tool use does not need a prewritten program. Judge only the actual attempted effect. Inquiries, offers and quoted claims do not establish acceptance, payment, transfer, repair or delivery. Speech can carry lies: delivery means the words were heard, not that they are true. Apply a property change only if the action can cause that specific change. Untouched facts remain unchanged. Partial work cannot imply all subsequent steps or long-term goals are complete. Observation reveals only information accessible through the attempted channel; a hidden identity or another person's thoughts remain hidden. Account for other simultaneous actions competing for the same resources. Code computes quantities and time. Do not use model confidence as randomness. Use unknown when the decisive evidence is absent.
The concrete action is its kind, selected parameters and actual utterance. proposal.rawText preserves the whole original request and proposal.goal preserves the larger intention; neither makes every requested step part of this event. For speak, judge whether the words can actually be spoken and heard, without requiring their truth, the listener's agreement or delivery of promised goods. "I accept" can be delivered before payment; a refusal can be successfully spoken. For observe, a null target means inspect the immediate surroundings, and self means inspect personal resources plus the nearby scene. An observation may succeed while finding no answer; visibility is judged separately. preparatoryFor means this is a discovery step before the indicated action. A deliveredEvidence record proves only that the actor perceived that content, with its original source and testimony status.
