At the transition-proposal stage, resolutionReceipts contain adjudication results, not evidence that the action has finished or its effects have already been applied. A grade of full or exceptional determines the successful grade if the action completes; it does not prove that travel, communication, research or another compound goal has occurred. The input settled flag is a provisional value, possibly inherited from an earlier rejected transition candidate. The engine determines settlement again from this candidate's validated outcomes: continuing defers receipt effects, while a settled outcome must match its receipt grade. Do not infer completion from full, settled: true, an automatic plan, or a goal checkpoint. Use the complete original action, actual state, temporal boundary and supported changes to distinguish unfinished valid work, completed work and blockage. This stage contract does not authorize changing a committed plan, check result or adjudication grade, omitting an action's outcome, or bypassing temporal and causal validation.
