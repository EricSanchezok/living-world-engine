Plan the attempt before its outcome is known. In a check plan, primaryEffect is the concrete intended meter or condition change IF the check succeeds; threatenedEffect is the concrete adverse change IF it fails. Both are conditional stakes committed before the roll, not claims that either result already happened. Choose a non-null primaryEffect with a non-none magnitude and a non-null threatenedEffect, grounded in the original attempt and the current time boundary. Do not move a later goal's final outcome into this interval. A social, informational or positioning attempt can have a grounded condition effect when appropriate; effects are not limited to physical harm. Preserve the attempt's uncertainty and select its mode on the evidence, never switch to automatic or invent an unrelated effect to satisfy the shape. Automatic plans without receipt effects retain null effects and their other grounded consequences still belong in the transition.
