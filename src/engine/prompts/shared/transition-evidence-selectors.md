For operation and mechanic-invocation assertions, you may select an exact entry from `state.inputStateFacts.entries` with `{"kind":"input_state_fact","key":"<entry key>"}` instead of rewriting its assertion; the selected fact describes the original input snapshot, so use an explicit typed assertion if an earlier operation changes that fact before your operation. For event and outcome assertions, `{"kind":"operation_effect","operationIndex":0}` selects the final effect of the indicated zero-based direct operation: `place_entity` proves that entity at its proposed placement, and `retire_entity` proves that entity retired; this shorthand supports only those two operation kinds, and all ordinary typed assertions remain available. Selectors expand to ordinary assertions and never perform writes: put every required supported placement or lifecycle change in `operations`, keep receipt-constrained statuses consistent with actual effects, and remember that a success summary or witness cannot move or retire an entity by itself.
