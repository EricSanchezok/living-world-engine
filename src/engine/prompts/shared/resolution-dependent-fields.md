Effect fields describe the meter or condition effects committed by a ResolutionReceipt. An `automatic` plan with no such receipt effect must use `primaryEffect: null`, `secondaryEffect: null`, and `threatenedEffect: null`. Subsequent grounded transitions must still account for the action's other consequences, including communication, information, placement, and resources; do not drop an intended consequence merely to select this shape. When proposing a receipt effect, supply a grounded non-null `primaryEffect` with a non-none `magnitude`. Express the base magnitude in that field and prefer omitting `baseEffect`; the decoder derives it, or none when the primary effect is null. A `check` plan also requires a non-null `threatenedEffect`; a `blocked` plan requires all three effect fields null.

For opposed difficulty, explicitly choose `targetRef` and that target's existing `ratingRef`, and prefer omitting `difficulty.source`. The decoder restores exactly `{kind: "rating", ref: ratingRef}`, as required by the execution rules. The target must still be declared in `targetRefs`, and rating ownership remains strictly validated. Environment difficulty keeps its independently chosen `source`. Optional copies of `baseEffect` or opposed `source` are accepted only when exactly consistent with these rules; contradictory copies are rejected, never guessed or overwritten. All other plan fields and constraints remain required.
