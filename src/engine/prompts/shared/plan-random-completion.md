For each initial plan, explicitly choose additionalRandomness: "none" or "defer". Consider the full original action, its interactions with the other actions assigned to this component, the visible world rules and every applicable authored random distribution. Choose "none" only when this action needs no additional discrete random request. Choose "defer" when any random request is needed, a decision depends on a check or random result, or you cannot determine that no request is needed. Do not change the action, plan mode, effects, time boundary or evidence to qualify for "none". This declaration concerns additional random commitment planning only: it never declares the action successful, completed, observed or committed. The engine may omit the subsequent request_random/done round only when all accepted initial plans declare "none", no new check or random results exist, and the original independent plan review accepts. Otherwise the existing continuation round remains available. This field is required only by the supplied initial-plan schema; do not add it to a scoped repair schema that does not request it.
