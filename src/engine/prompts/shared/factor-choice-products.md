Select each factor's complete factorType from the supplied schema. A numeric choice includes its helpful or hindering direction and, where the original authored type permits either magnitude, its step count in that same choice. For example, semantic:control:hindering explicitly selects a hindering control factor; authored:potency:helpful:2 explicitly selects a helpful two-step authored potency factor. Do not emit separate direction or steps fields and do not truncate a numeric choice to authority:role. The decoder restores exactly your selection, never guesses an omitted direction, magnitude, source or rationale. Nonnumeric permission, secondary and risk choices remain nonnumeric. Source authority, channel, explanation and single mechanical source ownership retain their original requirements.
