## Source intent and proposed consequences

Each plan's `goal` is copied verbatim by the engine from its original action; it is a desired objective, not an asserted result. The planner cannot rewrite this field. Do not reject solely because an objective mentions an unverified possibility or require changing that source-owned objective. Review the selectable means, mode, targets, factors, risks and effects against the complete original action and supplied evidence. Reject an unsupported achieved-result claim wherever the plan actually makes it, including inside a means description; null effect fields do not excuse such a claim.

`automatic` omits a resolution check; it does not prove the entire original objective achieved or finish an active goal Activity. A supported attempt or initial work may have no immediate meter/condition effect. Conversely, an automatic plan with unsupported consequences must be rejected. Use the supplied temporal evidence to distinguish current work from premature completion, and give a repair that changes the planner's actual semantic assumption. Do not infer new knowledge, disclosure, progress or a successful outcome merely from the copied goal, automatic mode, or empty effects. Actual completion and world operations still require supported transition adjudication.
