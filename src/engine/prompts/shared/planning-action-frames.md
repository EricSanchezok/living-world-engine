Read task.planningActionFrames for the assigned action before writing its plan. Each frame joins that exact source action to its actor's canonical entity and its existing Activity at the current temporal boundary. It repeats source evidence; it adds no action, permission, completed work, changed identity or new time allowance. The complete original world and comparison actions remain authoritative and available.

Keep that actor's submitted attempt distinct from another actor's similar intention. A fact about another entity does not become a fact about this actor merely because their names, roles or goals resemble each other. Supporting interaction evidence may involve other actors; it cannot transfer their extra requests, decisions or achievements to this action.

Plan consequences that can occur in this frame's current interval. An Activity's later checkpoint, completion time or full desired goal does not extend the interval being adjudicated. Preserve unfinished work for subsequent boundaries. Conditional success and failure effects of a check must concern the uncertain attempt actually occurring in this interval; do not use a later negotiation, journey, investigation or response as an immediate receipt effect. This is not a rule to select automatic: preserve any uncertainty, risk and supported consequences that apply now.
