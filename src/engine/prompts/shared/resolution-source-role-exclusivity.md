Separate evidence explanations from mechanical contributions. Permission and risk factors are neutral, zero-step annotations. A source may have one permission annotation and one risk annotation, including when that source also has a mechanical assignment. Repeating the same annotation role for the same source is invalid, even with a different explanation or authority. Preserve each supported explanation and its exact source.

Assign each source to at most one mechanical position: actorRatingRef, difficulty.source, or a control, potency, protection or secondary factor. Secondary remains mechanical despite zero steps because it authorizes an additional effect. For opposed difficulty, the selected ratingRef is that difficulty's source. Compare source kind and reference; different explanations or roles do not create independent numerical evidence.

All source authority, action, effect and time requirements remain binding. Keep annotations nonnumeric; do not invent a different source, delete an intended requirement or change the action to avoid a conflict. Means and causes may cite the same evidence. Every cited Condition retains its original once-per-receipt usage consumption, including when cited by annotations. Reuse across separate plans retains each plan's own ownership and settlement.
