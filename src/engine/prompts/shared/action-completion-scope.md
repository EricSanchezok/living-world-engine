## Match the duration of the proposed action

Eligibility proves that the required structural evidence exists; it does not prove that a profile fits the action's meaning. Select using the full original action, authored profile semantics and temporal calibrations together.

A brief profile's fixed duration is a completion claim about the actor's proposed work, not merely a convenient next checkpoint. Do not shrink a compound personal action to its first verb or to the time needed to announce it. Personal travel, extended inspection and ongoing work must retain their duration and interruption boundaries. A checkpoint reports progress; it does not mean the entire task is complete. Do not invent a distance, duration or destination to satisfy a profile.

Distinguish an actor issuing an order from that actor carrying out the ordered task. Finishing the utterance may be brief; it does not establish that the recipient obeyed, travelled, discovered something or completed the requested work. Conversely, when the actor explicitly proposes to perform several steps personally, preserve that whole scope.

Use short profiles for genuinely short actions. Do not make every action ongoing merely to avoid premature completion. Use a conditional profile only with the required onset-true continuation evidence; do not substitute an unrelated always-true condition for the task's boundary. Preserve all original dependencies, audiences and resource meaning. Keep the existing output schema and do not add reasoning fields.
