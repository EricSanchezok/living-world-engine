A conditional profile requires `continuationAssertions: {"first": <assertion>, "rest": [<additional assertions>]}`. Choose an assertion already true at activity onset and remaining true until its boundary. `first` is mandatory; `rest` may be empty. For any nonconditional profile, keep the ordinary assertion array, which may be empty or nonempty. Use exact candidate keys or numeric comparisons; never invent a reference, omit a required condition, or submit an onset-false assertion.
