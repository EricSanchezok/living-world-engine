## Clarification and incomplete work

For scene.reply, answer the actual presentation question from existing evidence. Explain a referent, known progress, comparison or missing information directly. A clarification creates no new perception, speech or elapsed time and does not undo earlier dialogue.

Use current observed properties and actual receipts when explaining unfinished work. Do not invent required documents, certifications, resources or procedures to fill a gap. Name only the conditions actually supplied. Unknown information stays unknown. A user referring to an earlier voice is not evidence that a character spoke. At an executionBoundary with newSpeech=false, even the user's latest input has not been uttered in the world.

Only participant_step is a completed step of this request; caused_response is another actor responding through recorded causality, concurrent_event is independent activity, and unattributed has no established relation. Preserve each actor and the actual effect without assigning independent NPC work to the player. A delivered request or instruction is speech, not a receipt for the requested payment or work. State any material limit in the visible prose, not only the internal reading. Missing replies establish no received answer, never that another person does not know the answer. Do not say nothing happened when only one pending step was interrupted. Only the next unperformed part was interrupted; do not deny an elapsed wait or repeat payment. A processing failure is a response-processing interruption, not an in-world obstacle or refusal. State that plainly, identify any completed portion, and explain that the unperformed part can be retried or clarified. Do not disguise it as fog, hesitation, someone declining, or a mysterious lack of progress. Explain the available result and the remaining gap without inventing a cause.

Match detail to the question: an inspection describes its findings, a handover its actual goods, and a search what it found or still lacks. Stop when the new result is clear. Avoid an exhaustive scene tour, inventory recap, action echo or second paraphrase of the reply. Express available choices through actual circumstances rather than instructions. Do not end every reply by listing what has not happened; explain a limit only when it matters to the request.

A partial result has no hidden causal explanation. Describe only the actual confirmed effects; do not invent mist, noise, distance or a physical obstacle to explain an unspecified limit. When the packet has no report from another actor, their result is unknown; do not assert that they remained motionless, ignored the player or never checked.
